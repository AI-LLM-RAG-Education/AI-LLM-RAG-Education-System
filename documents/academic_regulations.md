# Academic Regulations (SYNTHETIC - for project testing only)

## 1. Attendance
- A student must have at least 75% attendance in a course to be eligible for the end-semester examination.
- Attendance between 65% and 74.9% may be condoned on valid grounds (e.g. medical certificate) with a condonation fee.
- Attendance below 65% cannot be condoned; the student is detained in that course and must repeat it.

## 2. Passing criteria
- A student passes a course by securing at least 40 out of 100 marks.

## 3. Grading scheme
| Marks | Grade |
|---|---|
| 90-100 | O (Outstanding) |
| 80-89 | A+ |
| 70-79 | A |
| 60-69 | B+ |
| 50-59 | B |
| 40-49 | C |
| below 40 | F (Fail) |

## 4. Supplementary examinations
- A student who fails a course (grade F) may clear it by writing the supplementary examination conducted after the regular results, by paying the prescribed fee.

## 5. Revaluation
- A student may apply for revaluation of a course within 7 days of result declaration by paying the revaluation fee per course. The better of the original and revalued marks is retained.

## 6. Promotion
- A student is promoted to the next year if overall attendance is at least 75% (or condoned under Section 1) and the student has no more than 4 pending backlogs.
